How do I order?

Press Order on the plan you want. You sign in to your Zeiko Account (or make one), pick a system, name your VPS, choose a password, choose monthly, 6 months or yearly, and pay by QRIS. We make your VPS within 1–2 hours.

How much traffic is included?

500 GB of egress a month on every plan. Past that it is Rp5.000 per 500 GB.

Can I move to another plan?

To a bigger plan, any time: you pay the difference for the days left and it still ends on the same day. To a smaller one, choose it in your Zeiko Account and it starts at your next renewal, which is then priced at the smaller plan. Nothing changes until then, and the days you already paid for are not given back. Make sure what is on your VPS fits the smaller disk.

Can I reinstall it or change its password?

Yes. Ask for it in your Zeiko Account. We do it by hand, take a copy of the disk first when you reinstall, and answer under your VPS. A reinstall erases everything on the disk.

What if I forget to renew?

We email you 7, 3 and 1 day before your VPS ends, and you renew in your Zeiko Account. A day after it ends it is turned off; a week after, it is deleted with everything on it. Renew any time before that and it carries on as it was.

Can I end a plan early?

Yes, in your Zeiko Account. We delete the VPS and send you a refund for the days left, within 3 working days.

Do I get a free domain?

With 6 months or more, one .my.id, .biz.id or .web.id domain, free for its first year; after that Rp5.000 per 3 months with your renewals.

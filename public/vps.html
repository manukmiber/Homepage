Your address

You sign in to your VPS at an address of your own, not at the server’s. Every VPS starts on a free one, six random letters and digits under zeiko.biz.id, like k3x9qa.zeiko.biz.id.

  • A name of your own straight under zeiko.biz.id, like kantor.zeiko.biz.id: Rp3.000 per 3 months, nothing to connect.
  • Your own domain, like example.com: Rp5.000 per 3 months. You point it at your free address with a CNAME record.
  • A free domain with 6 months or more: one .my.id, .biz.id or .web.id domain, registered for you and free for its first 2 years, then Rp5.000 per 3 months with your renewals.

A name or a domain is paid 3 months at a time, in the same QRIS as your VPS. Changing it later is one price, once: Rp3.000 to a name, Rp5.000 to your own domain. It only changes where you sign in: SSH and Remote Desktop go straight to your VPS.

Questions, answered

How do I order?

Press Order on the plan you want. You sign in to your Zeiko Account (or make one), pick a system, name your VPS, choose a password, choose 1 to 12 months, and pay by QRIS. We make your VPS within 1–2 hours.

What do I get for paying 6 months or more?

No setup fee, a free .my.id, .biz.id or .web.id domain for its first 2 years, and 2 months free: pay 6 months and your VPS runs for 8, pay 12 and it runs for 14. The 2 free months come with every renewal of 6 months or more too. Under 6 months, setting it up is Rp10.000, once.

How much traffic is included?

500 GB of egress a month on every plan. Past that it is Rp5.000 per 500 GB.

Can I move to another plan?

To a bigger plan, any time: you pay the difference for the days left and it still ends on the same day. To a smaller one, choose it in your Zeiko Account and it starts at your next renewal, which is then priced at the smaller plan. Nothing changes until then, and the days you already paid for are not given back. Make sure what is on your VPS fits the smaller disk.

Can I reinstall it or change its password?

Yes. Ask for it in your Zeiko Account. We do it by hand, take a copy of the disk first when you reinstall, and answer under your VPS. A reinstall erases everything on the disk.

What if I forget to renew?

We email you 7, 3 and 1 day before your VPS ends, and you renew in your Zeiko Account. A day after it ends it is turned off; a week after, it is deleted with everything on it. Renew any time before that and it carries on as it was.

Can I end a plan early?

Yes, in your Zeiko Account. We delete the VPS and send you a refund for the days left, within 3 working days.

Can my VPS have a nicer address?

Yes. It starts on a free one under zeiko.biz.id; a name of your own there is Rp3.000 per 3 months, and your own domain Rp5.000 per 3 months. Pick it when you order, or change it later in your Zeiko Account.

Do I get a free domain?

With 6 months or more, one .my.id, .biz.id or .web.id domain, free for its first 2 years; after that Rp5.000 per 3 months with your renewals.

Is there support?

Yes, from people, 24/7, and free. Chat us on WhatsApp any time.

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to make a website?

No. Each package comes with a template in several designs: pick one, type your name, a few lines, your pictures and your WhatsApp number, change its fonts and colours if you like while the preview shows it on a phone and on a desktop, press Publish, and the page is made for you. If you can make one yourself, upload your own files instead.

How long does an event package stay online?

Until the day you pick when you order, up to six months ahead. You can keep it online longer from your Zeiko Account.

What happens when the year ends?

We email you 7, 3 and 1 day before. A day after it ends the website goes offline; a week after, its files are deleted. Renew any time before that and everything stays as it is.

What can I not put on it?

Anything against the law or our terms. A website taken down after a report stops being shown at once.

I need PHP or a database.

Take the learn-to-code package, which runs in cPanel, or Zeiko Hosting for WordPress and bigger sites.

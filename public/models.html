What the figures mean

Time to first token
How long until the first token of the answer arrives.
Latency
How long until the whole answer has arrived.
Output speed
Output tokens per second while the answer is being written.
Availability
The share of requests answered without a server error.
Cache rate
The share of input tokens read from cache, at the cache-read rate.
Calls per second
Pranata V1's answered calls, divided by the seconds in the window.

Medians over the window you pick, beside the same window just before it. Times are in UTC.

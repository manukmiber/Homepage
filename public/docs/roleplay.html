Roleplay models

Three models for roleplay and creative writing, from safe-for-work to unrestricted. Same context, same reply length, same API: they differ in what they will say, and in price.

Thinking effort

Each request can set how hard the model thinks before it answers, with reasoning_effort. Leave it out and the model thinks at high. The effort picks the output rate:

BandEffortsJagad output, per 1M tokens
No thinkingnone minimal$1.20
Standardlow medium high$1.50
Max thinkingmax$2.00

Reasoning tokens count as output tokens, at the band's rate, so asking for less thinking lowers both the rate and the number of tokens. Input and cache read cost the same at every effort. Every model's rates are on Pricing & plans.

To leave the reasoning text out of the reply, send "include_reasoning": false. It is still produced, and still counted.

What they are for

The roleplay models are for roleplay and the writing around it. Other requests, such as code, homework or general questions, get a short refusal. It comes back as an ordinary reply (HTTP 200, finish_reason: "stop") and costs a flat $0.05 in place of its token price.

Which one should I pick?

Jagad for safe-for-work roleplay, and when you are not sure. Asmarandana for adult roleplay, if you gate your own users: offer it only to people you have checked are adults. Wisanggeni when you need a model that rarely says no; it carries the highest rate.

Live performance

Time to first token, output speed, latency and availability for each model, over the last 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days, are on /models, updated every hour.

Getting a key

Email admin@zeiko.biz.id with the models you want and roughly what volume you expect. Your key arrives with everything you need to connect; the Chat API page covers the rest.

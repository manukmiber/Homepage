Troubleshooting

What an error means and what to do about it, and answers to the questions that come up most.

On this page

  • Errors
  • Replies
  • Keys and accounts
  • Still stuck?

Errors

404 model_not_found

The model ID has to be exactly as GET /v1/models lists it, all lowercase: jagad-512b-v1, not Jagad-512B-V1. The list is on API basics.

401 invalid_api_key

The key is missing or unknown. The header is Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY, with one space after Bearer. A pasted key sometimes brings a space or a line break with it. If you made a new key, the old one no longer works.

400 wrong_endpoint

Pranata V1 answers on /v1/decisions; the roleplay models on /v1/chat/completions.

402 insufficient_balance, 403 no_plan or 403 plan_expired

Nothing covers the call: no plan with requests left, and not enough balance. Buy a plan or add balance on Plans & balance.

429 plan_limit_reached

This period's plan requests are used, and your balance does not cover the call. Add balance to keep going now, or wait for the count to start again: your account's Overview says when.

Other 429 errors

Wait as many seconds as Retry-After says, then send the request again. Without the header, back off from about a second, doubling each time. See Retries and limits.

422 invalid_request from Pranata V1

error.param names the field that is wrong, such as questions.mood.criteria, and the message says what it should be. A call over 2,048 tokens is a 422 on state: split the state, or ask the long question in a call of its own.

413 context_length_exceeded

The conversation is longer than the model's 256K context. It is never cut short for you: drop or summarise the oldest messages and send it again.

The error is not JSON

A body that is not valid JSON, a missing Content-Type: application/json header, or a body over the size limit get a plain-text error. Check the error's Content-Type before parsing it.

Replies

The stream has lines that start with a colon

Those are comments that keep the connection open while the model works, such as : keep-alive. Skip every line that does not start with data:.

The reply is a short refusal

The roleplay models are for roleplay and the writing around it; code, homework and general questions get a short refusal at a flat $0.05. See what they are for.

The reply has the model's reasoning in it

Read message.content for the reply; the reasoning is in reasoning_content (or reasoning). To leave it out, send "include_reasoning": false.

The reply stops in the middle

When finish_reason is length, the reply reached max_tokens. Raise it, up to 32,000, or ask for a shorter reply. Thinking counts toward the length too: a lower reasoning_effort leaves more room for the reply.

How do I see what a request cost?

A chat reply says it in usage.cost, in USD. A Pranata V1 call is priced on usage.input_tokens, or counts against your plan; your account's Overview shows your calls and plan requests by day.

Keys and accounts

Can I call the API from a web page or a mobile app?

No. The key would be in the page or the app, where anyone can read it. Call the API from your own server, and have your page or app talk to that.

My key may have leaked

For Pranata V1, press Make a new key on API key: the old key stops working at once. For a roleplay key, write to admin@zeiko.biz.id.

Does my account's key call the roleplay models?

No: the account's key is for Pranata V1. Keys for the roleplay models are requested by email.

Is this the same account as Zeiko Chat?

Yes. One Zeiko Account signs you in to both, with one balance. See Your account.

Can I try Pranata V1 first?

Yes: the playground on the homepage gives 5 free tries every 30 minutes, with no account.

Still stuck?

Write to admin@zeiko.biz.id. Include the request's X-Request-Id, or, for an error, the time, the model and the status code.

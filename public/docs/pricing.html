Pricing & plans

Prices are in US dollars. Pranata V1 is priced per input token or by plan; the roleplay models are priced per token, with the output rate set by thinking effort.

Only input tokens are priced: the state and every question's text. Without a plan, calls come out of your balance at this price.

What counts as a request

  • A call of up to 1,000 tokens is one request; a call of 1,001 to 2,000 tokens is two. No call can be longer than 2,000 tokens.
  • A plan's requests are used first. After that, or once the plan has ended, calls come out of your balance at the per-token price.
  • When neither covers a call, the call is refused and nothing is used. Add balance or buy a plan to keep going.
  • Requests are counted per 30-day period of your plan. Your account's Overview shows what is left, and when the count starts again.

Reasoning tokens count as output tokens, at the output rate of the band the request landed in.

How a request's cost adds up

  1. Input tokens not read from cache, times the input rate.
  2. Input tokens read from cache, times the cache-read rate.
  3. Output tokens, reasoning included, times the output rate of the request's band.
  4. Add the three, and divide by one million.

Every reply says what it cost in usage.cost, and how much of its input was read from cache in usage.prompt_tokens_details.cached_tokens. For a quick estimate, use the cost estimator.

Refused requests

A refused request costs a flat $0.05 in place of its token price. See what the roleplay models are for.

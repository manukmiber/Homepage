Connect your domain

This page is for your own domain. Your site shows at it once the domain points at your hosting. There are two ways; you only need one. Both use details from your welcome email.

On this page

  • A free address or a name of yours
  • Way 1: change the nameservers
  • Way 2: point A records
  • SSL
  • Cloudflare CDN
  • More domains and subdomains
  • Check it worked

A free address or a name of yours

The free address (like k3x9qa.zeikosite.biz.id), or a name of your own like toko-bunga.zeiko.biz.id, needs nothing on this page: it is online, with SSL, as soon as your hosting is made. To move to your own domain later, open Hosting in your Zeiko Account and press Change the address, for Rp5.000 once (Your address); once we have moved it, point the domain here as below.

Way 1: change the nameservers

The easiest way. Your domain’s DNS moves here too and is managed in cPanel from then on, and email on your domain works straight away.

  1. Sign in where you bought your domainThe registrar: the place you renew the domain each year.
  2. Find the nameserversUsually under Nameservers, DNS or Manage domain.
  3. Use your own nameserversChoose the custom option, replace what is there with the two nameservers from your welcome email, and save.
  4. WaitUsually a few hours, at most 24 to 48, while the change spreads across the internet.

Way 2: point A records

Keep your DNS where it is, for example if your email already runs on it, and add these records there instead:

TypeNameValue
A@The IP address in your welcome email
AwwwThe same IP address

Remove any older A record for @ or www first, so only these are left. Email on your domain stays where it is; to use the email that comes with your plan, use way 1.

SSL

Once your domain points here, turn on SSL for it in cPanel:

  1. Open Let’s EncryptIn cPanel, under Security.
  2. Issue a certificatePress Issue beside your domain, then Issue again on the next page.
  3. Wait 5 to 10 minutesThe certificate installs by itself.

Then send every visitor to https://. In File Manager, turn on Show Hidden Files under Settings, open .htaccess in your site’s folder (or create it), and add at the top:

.htaccess
RewriteEngine On
RewriteCond %{HTTPS} off
RewriteRule ^ https://%{HTTP_HOST}%{REQUEST_URI} [L,R=301]

A WordPress site can skip this: in WordPress, set both addresses under Settings → General to start with https://.

Cloudflare CDN

Cloudflare CDN serves your pages from close to your visitors and shields your site from attacks. It comes with every plan: chat us on WhatsApp +62 819-3241-6096 (08.00–22.00 WIB) with the domain you want it on, and we set it up with you.

More domains and subdomains

Your plan holds as many websites as it says. To add one, open Domains in cPanel, press Create A New Domain, type the domain, and untick Share document root so it gets a folder of its own. Then point that domain here the same way.

A subdomain, like shop.yourdomain.com, is added the same way. With way 1 it needs nothing more; with way 2, add an A record for it too.

Check it worked

Open your domain in a browser. Before you upload anything you see a blank or placeholder page instead of an error: the domain points here. Still seeing something else after 48 hours? Chat us on WhatsApp +62 819-3241-6096 (08.00–22.00 WIB) with the domain.

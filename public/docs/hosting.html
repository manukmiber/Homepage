Hosting basics

Zeiko Hosting runs WordPress and PHP sites, and Node.js, Python and Ruby apps, all managed in cPanel. This page covers what a plan gives you, how to order, and what you get once your hosting is ready. Plans and prices are on the Hosting page.

What a plan gives you

Each plan hasWhat it means
WebsitesHow many domains you can run sites on: your main domain, and the rest added in cPanel.
RAMMemory your sites and apps can use at once.
NVMe storageSpace for your sites’ files, databases and email, on fast NVMe drives.
Object storageExtra space for files, apart from your web storage, on Small and up.
PricePer month or per year, in rupiah. A year costs the same as ten months; the first month of Small and Medium costs less on your first hosting.

Every plan also has SSL, unlimited MySQL databases, email on your own domain, jailed SSH, PHP Speedboost on LiteSpeed, Imunify360, Cloudflare CDN, Softaculous, and app setup for Node.js, Python and Ruby.

500 GB of egress a month, what your site sends to its visitors, is free on every plan. Past that, every GB is Rp10 (Rp5.000 per 500 GB). Hosting in your Zeiko Account shows what you have used this month; once a month is over, what went past 500 GB is added to your next renewal.

Your address

Every hosting is online at an address of its own as soon as it is made. You pick one of four when you order:

AddressPriceWhat to do
Free, like k3x9qa.zeikosite.biz.idFreeNothing: six random letters and digits are made for you, with SSL.
A name of your own, like toko-bunga.zeiko.biz.idRp3.000 per 3 monthsNothing: it is online as soon as your hosting is.
Your own domain, like example.comRp5.000 per 3 monthsPoint it here with the nameservers from your welcome email: Connect your domain.
A free domain, like toko-bunga.my.id (with 6 months or more)Free for its first year, then Rp5.000 per 3 monthsNothing: we register it and connect it for you.

A name or a domain is paid 3 months at a time, in the same QRIS as your hosting: the first 3 months come with your order, and after that the renewal that needs them carries the next 3 (by the year, a year of them). To change the address later, open Hosting in your Zeiko Account and press Change the address: it is one price, once — Rp3.000 to a name, Rp5.000 to your own domain — and covers the new address to the end of your paid time. We move it within 1–2 hours; your files stay as they are.

Ordering

  1. Pick a planOn the Hosting page, press Order. Sign in to your Zeiko Account, or make one, and confirm your email: your hosting details are sent there.
  2. Pick your addressThe free one, a name of your own, or your own domain (see Your address), and choose monthly or yearly.
  3. Pay by QRISScan the QR with your banking app or any app that pays QRIS, pay the exact amount it shows, and press I’ve paid.
  4. We check your paymentBy hand, usually in 1–5 minutes.
  5. We make your hostingWithin 1–2 hours, then we send your welcome email.

Your welcome email

Once your hosting is ready, we email you everything you need. Keep it somewhere safe.

Change the password in cPanel after you first sign in.

Renewing

Hosting in your Zeiko Account shows until when each plan runs. Renew a month or a year there, by QRIS, whenever you like: the new period starts where the current one ends, so renewing early loses nothing. We email you 7, 3 and 1 day before it ends.

  1. On the day it endsYour site is still on. Renew today and nothing changes.
  2. 1 day afterIt is suspended: your site goes offline, and your files, databases and email are kept.
  3. 7 days afterIt is deleted, with its files, databases and email, for good.

Renewed while suspended, it comes back as it was: we lift the suspension within 1–2 hours.

A renewal asks the plan’s price, plus the next 3 months of a name or a domain when they are due, plus any egress past the free 500 GB a month. Hosting in your Zeiko Account shows the whole amount on each button before you pay.

cPanel, your control panel

Everything about your hosting is done in cPanel. The parts you will use most:

  • File Manager: upload, edit and unzip your site’s files.
  • Domains: add more domains and subdomains, up to your plan’s number of websites.
  • Softaculous Apps Installer: WordPress and other apps in one click.
  • MySQL Databases and phpMyAdmin: make databases and look inside them.
  • Email Accounts: addresses on your domain, read in webmail or any mail app.
  • Let’s Encrypt: SSL for your domains.
  • Setup Node.js App, Setup Python App and Setup Ruby App: run your apps.
  • Select PHP Version: PHP 5 to 8, and its extensions.
  • Terminal and SSH Access: a shell in your browser, or from your own computer.

How to do each is in Put your site online.

Static sites

A site that is only HTML, CSS, JavaScript and images, with nothing running on the server, can go on static hosting instead of a plan: Rp1.000 per GB a month. The school and business packages on Zeiko Sites are static sites too, from a template you fill in.

  1. OrderIn your Zeiko Account, open Sites, pick a package or Static website by the GB, and pay by QRIS. Once the payment is checked, the website is online at its free address.
  2. Fill it inA package’s Edit the page opens its template: pick one of its designs, type your words, choose your pictures, change the fonts and colours if you like, check the preview on a phone and on a desktop, and press Save and publish. Or press Files and upload a folder: its index.html is the front page, and every other file keeps its path.
  3. Your own domainAt your domain’s DNS, add a CNAME record from your domain (or www) to the free address the website’s card shows. HTTPS follows by itself within a few minutes.

What you may not host

  • Pornography, in any form.
  • Online gambling.
  • Phishing and other scam sites.
  • Spam, mass-mailing scripts, and tools for breaking into other systems.
  • Files you have no right to share.
  • Web storage used as a dump for backups or media; object storage, or Zeiko Drive, is for that.

A site that breaks these rules is suspended without notice.

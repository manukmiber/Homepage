Hosting basics

Zeiko Hosting runs WordPress and PHP sites, and Node.js, Python and Ruby apps, all managed in cPanel. This page covers what a plan gives you, how to order, and what you get once your hosting is ready. Plans and prices are on the Hosting page.

On this page

  • What a plan gives you
  • Ordering
  • Your welcome email
  • Renewing
  • cPanel, your control panel
  • Static sites
  • What you may not host

What a plan gives you

Each plan hasWhat it means
WebsitesHow many domains you can run sites on: your main domain, and the rest added in cPanel.
RAMMemory your sites and apps can use at once.
NVMe storageSpace for your sites’ files, databases and email, on fast NVMe drives.
Object storageExtra space for files, apart from your web storage, on Small and up.
PricePer month or per year, in rupiah. A year costs the same as ten months; the first month of Mini, Small and Medium costs less on your first hosting.

Every plan also has SSL, unlimited MySQL databases, email on your own domain, unlimited bandwidth, jailed SSH, PHP Speedboost on LiteSpeed, Imunify360, Cloudflare CDN, Softaculous, and app setup for Node.js, Python and Ruby.

Ordering

  1. Pick a planOn the Hosting page, press Order. Sign in to your Zeiko Account, or make one, and confirm your email: your hosting details are sent there.
  2. Type your domainAnd choose monthly or yearly. No domain yet? Write to admin@zeiko.biz.id first.
  3. Pay by QRISScan the QR with your banking app or any app that pays QRIS, pay the exact amount it shows, and press I’ve paid.
  4. We check your paymentBy hand, usually in 1–5 minutes.
  5. We make your hostingWithin 15–60 minutes, then we send your welcome email.

Your welcome email

Once your hosting is ready, we email you everything you need. Keep it somewhere safe.

In the emailWhat it is for
cPanel addressWhere you sign in to manage your hosting.
Username and passwordYour cPanel sign-in. The same username is used for FTP and SSH.
Two nameserversWhat you put at your domain to point it here. See Connect your domain.
IP addressFor pointing your domain with A records instead of nameservers.
SSH portFor connecting over SSH from your own computer.

Change the password in cPanel after you first sign in.

Renewing

Hosting in your Zeiko Account shows until when each plan runs. Renew a month or a year there, by QRIS, whenever you like: the new period starts where the current one ends, so renewing early loses nothing. We email you 7, 3 and 1 day before it ends.

  1. On the day it endsYour site is still on. Renew today and nothing changes.
  2. 1 day afterIt is suspended: your site goes offline, and your files, databases and email are kept.
  3. 7 days afterIt is deleted, with its files, databases and email, for good.

Renewed while suspended, it comes back as it was: we lift the suspension within 15–60 minutes.

cPanel, your control panel

Everything about your hosting is done in cPanel. The parts you will use most:

  • File Manager: upload, edit and unzip your site’s files.
  • Domains: add more domains and subdomains, up to your plan’s number of websites.
  • Softaculous Apps Installer: WordPress and other apps in one click.
  • MySQL Databases and phpMyAdmin: make databases and look inside them.
  • Email Accounts: addresses on your domain, read in webmail or any mail app.
  • Let’s Encrypt: SSL for your domains.
  • Setup Node.js App, Setup Python App and Setup Ruby App: run your apps.
  • Select PHP Version: PHP 5 to 8, and its extensions.
  • Terminal and SSH Access: a shell in your browser, or from your own computer.

How to do each is in Put your site online.

Static sites

A site that is only HTML, CSS, JavaScript and images, with nothing running on the server, can go on static hosting instead of a plan: Rp1.000 per GB a month. Order it the same way, with Static site as the plan and roughly how many GB it is; we reply with how to upload it.

What you may not host

  • Pornography, in any form.
  • Online gambling.
  • Phishing and other scam sites.
  • Spam, mass-mailing scripts, and tools for breaking into other systems.
  • Files you have no right to share.
  • Web storage used as a dump for backups or media; object storage, or Zeiko Drive, is for that.

A site that breaks these rules is suspended without notice.

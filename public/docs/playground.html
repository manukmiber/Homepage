Playground

Ask Pranata V1 a real question on the homepage and see its answer, with a probability for every option. No account and no code needed.

On this page

  • Free tries
  • Pick a kind of question
  • The fields
  • Reading the answer
  • From the playground to the API

Free tries

5 free tries every 30 minutes. Under the Ask Pranata V1 button, the playground says how many are left and when the next one comes back. It needs JavaScript.

Pick a kind of question

TabWhat you fill inWhat comes back
Yes or noThe text, the question, and, if you like, what yes and no mean.The probability of yes and of no.
Pick oneThe text, the question, and the options, one per line (2 to 8).Every option with its probability; the top one is marked.
Score itThe text, the question, and the levels, one per line, lowest first (2 to 10).Every level with its probability; the top one is marked.

Each tab starts with a made-up example. Change it, or write your own.

The fields

FieldUp toWhat to put in it
Text to judge4,000 charactersWhat Pranata V1 reads: a message, a reply, a review, a listing.
Question500 charactersWhat you want to know about it. One thing per question.
Yes means / No means120 characters eachOptional. A short description of each answer sharpens it.
Options2 to 8 linesThe choices, one per line.
Levels2 to 10 linesThe scale, one level per line, lowest first.

Reading the answer

The probabilities add up to 100%. A clear winner, say 90% or more, is a confident answer. A near-even split means the text does not settle the question: rewording the question, or describing what each answer means, usually helps.

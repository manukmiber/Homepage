PDF tools

Sign a letter, fill in a form, join and split files, or make a PDF lighter, without leaving the browser.

On this page

  • Opening PDFs
  • Pages
  • Fill & sign
  • Form
  • Tools

Opening PDFs

  • PDF tools in a PDF's menu. Select several PDFs first to join them: the menu says Merge 2 PDFs and so on.
  • Make a PDF for one or more photos puts them into one PDF, a page each.
  • A PDF locked with a password asks for it, and can then only be viewed.

The tabs: View, Pages, Fill & sign, Form (only when the PDF has fields) and Tools. Undo sits at the top.

Pages

Tap pages to select them (Select all for every page), then:

ButtonWhat it does
Rotate left / Rotate rightTurns the selected pages a quarter.
Move earlier / Move laterChanges their order.
DuplicatePuts a copy of each right after it, with anything placed on it.
DeleteTakes them out (at least one page stays).
Take out selectedSaves only those pages as a new PDF.
Split into pagesSaves every page as its own PDF.
Blank pageAdds an empty page, the size of the selected one, after it.
Add a PDF / Add picturesAdds pages from other PDFs or photos at the end.

Fill & sign

Choose what to put on a page, then tap the page where it goes. Once placed, drag it to move it, drag its corner to resize it, or press × to remove it.

ButtonWhat it puts on the page
SignatureDraw it with a finger or mouse, type your name, or take a photo of it on white paper (the paper is removed). Remember on this device keeps it for next time.
Text, DateWords you type, or today's date.
Tick, CrossA ✓ or ✗, for boxes on paper forms.
HighlightDrag across text to mark it in yellow; the text stays readable.
White-outDrag to cover part of the page with white.
PictureA logo or a company stamp from your device.

The coloured dots set the ink for text, dates, ticks and crosses: black, blue or red. Signatures are black or blue.

White-out only covers what you see. The text under it can still be copied out of the PDF. To really remove it, save first, then use Tools → Make it smaller → Smallest, which turns every page into a picture.

Form

When a PDF has fields to fill in, the Form tab lists them page by page: text boxes, tick boxes and choices. Fill them in and save; the answers go into the PDF's own fields, so they can still be changed in any PDF app. Tick Lock the answers to make them part of the page instead.

Joining, reordering or deleting pages locks the answers by itself, so they are never lost. Form fields only take Latin letters.

Tools

ToolWhat it does
WatermarkSlanted words across the middle of every page, such as CONFIDENTIAL or SAMPLE. Set the words, the strength and the size; you see it on the pages while you work.
Page numbersBottom centre, bottom right or top right, as “1”, “1 / 10” or “Page 1 of 10”, starting from the number you choose.
Make it smallerBalanced shrinks the photos inside and keeps the text selectable; Smallest turns every page into a picture. If the result is not smaller, it says so.
Turn into picturesEvery page as a JPG or PNG, at 150 or 300 dpi, signatures and marks included.
Get the textAll the text as one .txt file, page by page. A scanned PDF has no text to take out.

Everything in Tools works on the PDF as it would be saved now, with what you placed and filled in.

Video editor

Cut the start and end off a clip, take out the bits you don't want, or join several videos into one, in the browser.

On this page

  • Opening videos
  • Cutting
  • Saving: Fast or Precise

Opening videos

Edit video in a video's menu. Select several videos first to join them: the menu says Join 3 videos and so on. Add video inside the editor adds more clips at the end.

Cutting

The timeline shows your video as parts. Move the marker to a moment, then:

ButtonWhat it does
Play / PausePlays from the marker.
Start here / End hereTrims the selected part so it starts or ends at the marker.
Split hereCuts the part in two at the marker.
Delete partTakes the selected part out.
Move left / Move rightChanges the order of the parts.

Each part shows where it starts and ends and how long it is. Undo works step by step. If the browser can't play a video it can still cut and save it.

Saving: Fast or Precise

WayGood for
FastNo re-encoding, so the quality stays exactly the same and it is quick. Cuts snap to the nearest key frame, so they can be slightly off. Only joins videos of the same format and size.
PreciseRe-encodes: cuts are frame-exact, and videos of different sizes can be joined. Takes longer, and needs a browser that can encode video.

Then choose Join into one file, or Each part as its own file, and press Make the video.

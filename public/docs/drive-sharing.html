Sharing & downloads

Share any file or folder with a link, send it where people are, and download many files at once as one archive.

On this page

  • Share links
  • Sending a link
  • Downloading many files
  • Compressing photos and videos
  • Family plans
  • Local ads
  • Reporting a link

Share links

Pick a file or folder and press Share. Anyone with the link can download it, with no sign-in. In Link settings you choose:

SettingChoices
Works for1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, or for ever
PasswordOptional: whoever opens the link types it first
Download limitOptional: the link stops after that many downloads

Turn off link stops it at once; Make a new link gives a new address and the old one stops working. Shared lists every link with how often it was downloaded and whether it still works. While your Drive is read-only, new links cannot be made.

Sending a link

From the share window: copy the link, show it as a QR code, or send it through WhatsApp, Telegram or email, with a message of your own if you like. Email goes to the addresses you type, one message each.

Downloading many files

Select files or a folder and press Download as an archive:

FormatGood for
ZIP, fastPhotos, videos and anything already compressed. The quickest.
ZIP, compressedDocuments and text, which shrink.
TAR.ZSTThe smallest archive, when it is offered. Opens with 7-Zip ZS, PeaZip or ZArchiver.

An archive too big to make on the spot is prepared first: Drive tells you when it is ready, and it waits under Downloads for 24 hours.

Compressing photos and videos

  • Pick photos or videos and press Compress. Choose the format, the quality (Smallest, Balanced or High) and whether to save the result as a new copy or replace the original.
  • Each file costs compress credits: your plan includes some every month, and compress packs add more. A long video takes one credit per minute.
  • If the result is not smaller, nothing changes and the credit comes back.
  • GPS location and camera details are always taken out of the result.
  • Compress without saving to Drive takes a photo or video from your device, gives back the smaller one, and deletes both after 24 hours without using your space.

Family plans

A family plan shares one plan's space among several people. Everyone keeps a private My Drive, and the family shares a Family Drive. The owner invites members by link or email (an invitation works once, for 7 days), can set how much space each may use, and sees only how much each member uses — never what is in their My Drive. Family plans show on the Drive page when they are on sale.

Local ads

Drive may show one small, quiet ad for a business in your city: under the space meter, and on share pages. Never while you upload or download, and with no trackers. You can hide an ad or report it.

  • Which city. The one on your Zeiko Account, asked when you sign up; change it in your account's Settings. Drive never guesses where you are: without a city you see ads for all of Indonesia.
  • Advertising your business. Chat WhatsApp +62 819-3241-6096 with your business, the city and the dates you want; Advertise in Drive says the same. We set the ad up with you.

Reporting a link

If a Zeiko link shares something it should not, open it and press Report, or use the report form in Drive. Every report is read by the Zeiko team; urgent ones within 4 hours.

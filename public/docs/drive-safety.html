Safety & privacy

What Drive does to keep you and the people you share with safe, and what stays private.

On this page

  • The safety check
  • Editing stays on your device
  • Safe sharing
  • Reporting abuse
  • Notifications
  • Your account and your data

The safety check

Every upload is checked automatically before it can be downloaded or shared. Its state shows on the file and in Details:

StateWhat it means
Being checkedJust uploaded. Usually a few seconds; it cannot be shared yet.
SafeNothing harmful was found.
Harmful / Blocked (harmful)It matched something harmful. It stays in your Drive but cannot be downloaded or shared, and you get a notification.
Not fully checkedPart of it could not be looked into (for example a password-protected archive).
Check failedThe check could not finish. It is tried again.

No person opens your files for this: the check is automatic.

Editing stays on your device

The photo, video and PDF editors, documents, spreadsheets, text files and the ZIP viewer all work inside your browser. The file is not sent anywhere while you edit: only when you press Save to Drive does the result go up, like any upload. Download to this device never touches Drive at all.

Saved photos lose their location (GPS) and camera details.

Safe sharing

  • A share link can have a password, an end date (Works for) and a download limit, and you can Turn off link or Make a new link at any time.
  • Only files that passed the safety check can be shared.
  • Shared lists every link you made and whether it still works.

More in Sharing & downloads.

Reporting abuse

Found something harmful behind a Zeiko link, or an ad that breaks the rules? Open Report on the shared page (or Report abuse at the bottom of Drive's policy pages), paste the link, pick what is wrong and press Send report. Reports are read by the Zeiko team; urgent ones (child abuse, terrorism, violence) are handled within 4 hours. You can leave a way to reach you, or not.

Notifications

The bell in Drive always shows notifications. In Settings you choose which ones also come by email: a file blocked by the safety check, space almost or completely full, renewal reminders, plan changes, family invitations, a ready archive, finished compressing and ad status. Renewal reminders and receipts always come by email.

Your account and your data

  • One sign-in. Drive uses your Zeiko Account; signing out there signs you out of Drive too. Keep its password to yourself.
  • Your copy. Download my data and Download all my files in Settings give you everything.
  • Leaving. Deleting your Drive in Settings removes it and its files for good a few days later; you can cancel until then.
  • The rules. Drive's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are linked from Drive's sign-in page and its side menu.

Your Zeiko Account

One Zeiko Account signs you in to ZeikoAI, Zeiko Chat and Zeiko Drive. It holds your Pranata V1 plan, your balance and your API key.

One account, three products

  • One sign-in. The same email and password, or one-click sign-in, works here, in Zeiko Chat and in Zeiko Drive. Signed in, your name is at the top of every page of this site.
  • Signed in once, signed in everywhere. Zeiko Drive opens already signed in, and follows this site: sign out here and Drive is signed out on this browser too; sign in as someone else and Drive switches with you.
  • One balance. Add balance once: it covers Pranata V1 calls past your plan and Zeiko Chat replies past your chat plan. Zeiko Drive does not use it: each purchase there is made in the Drive app (Drive plans).
  • Every plan in one place. Pranata V1 plans and Zeiko Chat plans are bought side by side on Plans & balance.

Create an account

  1. Open Create account.
  2. Use a one-click sign-in, or type your email, a username, your city and a password.
    • Username: 3 to 24 letters, numbers or _. It shows as @username.
    • City: where you live. Zeiko Drive shows local ads for it; nothing else uses it. The postal code beside it is optional.
    • Password: 8 characters or more.
  3. Have a referral code? Open Have a referral code? and type it. A code that came in a link (?ref=) is filled in for you, and remembered on that browser for 30 days.
  4. Press Create account.

Confirm your email

We send a 6-digit code to your address. Type it into the Confirm your email banner at the top of your account page, or open the link in the email; both work for 48 hours. Send a new code sends another, a minute after the last one.

Confirm it early: it is where we send a link if you ever forget your password.

Plans and balance

  • A Pranata V1 plan gives a set number of requests for 30 days. See the plans.
  • A plan's requests are used first; after that, calls come out of your balance.
  • Requests are counted per 30-day period of your plan, not per calendar month. The Overview shows when the count starts again.
  • Buying the plan you are on adds 30 days to it. Buying a different plan starts it at once, and what is left of the old one goes back to your balance.
  • Your balance is the same balance in Zeiko Chat.

Signed in, the buying guide walks you through finishing a purchase, step by step.

Your API key

  • Open API key and press Show key. Copy copies it, and the Address under it is the base URL to send it to.
  • The key is hidden again as soon as you leave the page.
  • Models this key can call lists each model with its price and its longest call. The account's key is for Pranata V1; keys for the roleplay models are requested by email.
  • Make a new key if the old one may have been seen by someone else. The old key stops working at once, so update your apps straight after.

Keep your key private. Anyone who has it can use your plan and your balance. Keep it on your server: never in a web page, a mobile app or a public repository.

Settings

  • Profile: your username, name, email and city, and the referral code you signed up with.
  • Where you live: change your city and postal code. An account made with a one-click sign-in, or before the city was asked, is asked once by a banner on the account page.
  • Sign-in methods: which are connected; connect another one-click sign-in here.
  • Password: change it, or set one if you signed up with a one-click sign-in, so your email signs you in too.
  • Change email: needs your password. The new address works once you confirm it with the code we send there.
  • Sign out: signs you out on this device, here and in Zeiko Drive.

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, press Forgot it? and type your email. If it has an account, a link to choose a new password is on its way. The link works once, for 30 minutes, and the new password works here, in Zeiko Chat and in Zeiko Drive.

Usage

The Overview charts your calls per day over the last 30 days (UTC), with the plan requests they counted as and the input tokens they carried. Show as a table lists the same numbers day by day.

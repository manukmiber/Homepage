Your Zeiko Account

One Zeiko Account signs you in to Zeiko Sites, Hosting, VPS and Drive. Everything you order is in it, each product on its own tab.

On this page

  • One account for everything
  • Create an account
  • Confirm your email
  • Your account pages
  • Settings
  • Forgot your password?

One account for everything

  • One sign-in. The same email and password, or one-click sign-in, works here, in Zeiko Drive and in Zeiko Chat. Signed in, your name is at the top of every page of this site.
  • Signed in once, signed in everywhere. Zeiko Drive opens already signed in, and follows this site: sign out here and Drive is signed out on this browser too; sign in as someone else and Drive switches with you.
  • Everything in one place. Your hosting, your VPS and your websites are ordered, renewed and upgraded on their own tabs. Zeiko Drive is bought in the Drive app itself (Drive plans).

Create an account

  1. Open Create account.
  2. Use a one-click sign-in, or type your email, a username, your city and a password.
    • Username: 3 to 24 letters, numbers or _. It shows as @username.
    • City: where you live. Zeiko Drive shows local ads for it; nothing else uses it. The postal code beside it is optional.
    • Password: 8 characters or more.
  3. Have a referral code? Open Have a referral code? and type it. A code that came in a link (?ref=) is filled in for you, and remembered on that browser for 30 days.
  4. Press Create account.

Confirm your email

We send a 6-digit code to your address. Type it into the Confirm your email banner at the top of your account page, or open the link in the email; both work for 48 hours. Send a new code sends another, a minute after the last one.

Confirm it early: it is where we send a link if you ever forget your password.

Settings

  • Profile: your username, name, email and city, and the referral code you signed up with.
  • Where you live: change your city and postal code. An account made with a one-click sign-in, or before the city was asked, is asked once by a banner on the account page.
  • Sign-in methods: which are connected; connect another one-click sign-in here.
  • Password: change it, or set one if you signed up with a one-click sign-in, so your email signs you in too.
  • Change email: needs your password. The new address works once you confirm it with the code we send there.
  • Sign out: signs you out on this device, here and in Zeiko Drive.

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, press Forgot it? and type your email. If it has an account, a link to choose a new password is on its way. The link works once, for 30 minutes, and the new password works here, in Zeiko Chat and in Zeiko Drive.

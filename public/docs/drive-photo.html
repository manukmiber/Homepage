Photo editor

A full photo editor in your browser, laid out like the phone apps you know: ready-made Looks, and a set of tools you open one at a time.

On this page

  • How it works
  • Looks
  • The tools
  • Tips

How it works

  1. Open the photoEdit photo in the file's menu, or Tools → Edit photo for a photo on your device.
  2. Pick a Look, or a toolThe two tabs at the bottom: Looks and Tools.
  3. Apply or cancelA tool opens under the photo. ✓ keeps the change as one step, ✕ drops it.
  4. SaveAs a new file, in place of the original, or to your device (saving).

At the top: Undo and Redo go back and forward one step at a time, and holding the eye button shows the photo as it came, in the same frame. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z work too.

Looks

Fifteen ready-made styles, each shown on your own photo: Original, Pop, Bright, Morning, Faded, Film, Vintage, Drama, HDR, Glow, Cool, Warm, Black & white, Noir and Sepia. Tap one to use it, then set its Strength. A Look adds to your own changes from the tools, so you can use both.

The tools

ToolWhat it does
Tune imageBrightness, Contrast, Saturation, Ambiance (balances light and livens muted colours), Highlights, Shadows and Warmth. Pick one at the top, move the slider. Auto suggests settings for your photo.
DetailsStructure brings out texture (or, below 0, smooths it); Sharpening makes edges crisp.
CurvesDrag the line to shape light and dark, for all colours or red, green or blue alone, over the photo's histogram. Tap the line to add a point, double-tap a point to remove it. Ready-made curves: Straight, Contrast, Strong contrast, Brighter, Darker, Matte.
White balanceTemperature (bluer to yellower) and Tint (greener to more magenta). Auto makes the average colour neutral.
CropDrag the box or its corners. Shapes: Free, Original, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16, 4:5.
RotateTurn left or right, flip left-right or upside down, and Straighten up to 45° either way with a grid to line things up. The photo grows just enough that no empty corners show.
HealingTap a spot, blemish or small object: it is patched with the area around it that matches best. Set the brush size first; Remove all spots starts over.
Lens blurRound or Band: keeps one part sharp and blurs the rest, like a camera with a wide lens. Drag the circle on the photo to move the sharp part; set the blur strength, centre size, transition and (for a band) its angle.
VignetteDarker (or lighter) edges. Drag the circle to move the centre.
EffectsGlow (soft, bright light), Film grain and Fade (lifted blacks).
TextAdd text, type it, choose Plain, Bold, Box or Outline, a colour and a size, and drag it where you want. Text moves with the photo when you crop or rotate.
FramesThin, Thick, Polaroid or Round corners, in the colour and thickness you choose.
SizeScale (100%, 75%, 50%, 25%) or an exact width or height in pixels, the format (JPEG, PNG, WebP) and the quality.

Tips

  • Double-tap a slider to put it back where it started. Reset in a tool clears that tool.
  • Small screen? The photo you see is a lighter copy; the saved photo is made at full size, and looks the same.
  • Bigger photos take longer to save, a few seconds on a phone with many tools used.
  • Location and camera details are not kept in the saved photo.

Drive questions

Something not working the way you expect? Find the message you see, or the question you have.

On this page

  • Signing in
  • Uploads
  • Files and links
  • Space and plans
  • Editors
  • On a phone

Signing in

Drive keeps sending me to sign in.

Drive follows your Zeiko Account. Sign in on your account page, confirm your email if it asks, then open Drive again. In a private window, sign in there too.

Drive opened as somebody else.

It shows whoever is signed in to your Zeiko Account on this browser. Sign out on your account page and sign in as yourself.

“Your session has ended. Please sign in again.”

You signed out somewhere else, or it has been a long time. Sign in again; nothing in your Drive changes.

Uploads

An upload says Waiting for a long time.

Only a few files go up at once; it starts when one ahead of it finishes. A very big file ahead of it takes a while.

The upload stopped when I closed the page.

Open Drive again on the same device: it offers to pick up where it stopped (Resume).

It says I'm offline.

“You're offline. We'll try again by ourselves.” Uploads carry on when the connection is back.

I can't upload at all.

Uploading needs a plan with free space. Check the space meter, or open Plans.

Files and links

A file says Being checked and can't be shared.

Wait a few seconds: files can be shared once they are Safe. See the safety check.

A file says Blocked (harmful).

It matched something harmful, so it cannot be downloaded or shared. If you think that is wrong, write to us from the contact on the homepage.

Somebody says my link doesn't work.

The page they see says why: it has ended, reached its download limit, been turned off, or needs a password. Open Shared to change it or make a new link.

I deleted something by mistake.

Open Trash and press Restore, as long as Trash has not been emptied.

Space and plans

My space is full.

Empty Trash first (files in it still count), delete what you don't need, or press Get more space. See Drive plans.

What happens if my plan runs out?

That is explained in Drive plans, together with renewing and moving up or down.

Editors

“This file is too big to edit in the browser.”

Each editor has a size limit, so a phone doesn't run out of memory; the message says what it is. Download the file and use an app on your computer for very big files.

My HEIC photo won't open.

Only some browsers can open HEIC. Try Safari, or change it to JPEG first.

The video editor only offers Fast.

This browser can't encode video yet. Fast still cuts and joins without losing quality; Precise needs a newer browser.

A text file says it isn't UTF-8.

The editor won't open it rather than break its letters. Open it in a desktop app that can change its encoding to UTF-8.

Text in a PDF form won't save.

PDF forms can only take Latin letters. For other scripts, put it on the page as Text in Fill & sign instead.

On a phone

Can I use Drive on my phone?

Yes, in the phone's browser: everything here works there, including the editors. Long-press a file to select several.

Is there an app to install?

Drive runs in the browser; there is nothing to install.

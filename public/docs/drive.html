Drive basics

Zeiko Drive keeps your files: upload at full speed, find them again, share them with a link. It runs on the same Zeiko Account as Zeiko Hosting, VPS and Sites.

  • Open Drive

Open Drive

Drive is at drive.zeiko.biz.id. There is no separate Drive sign-up: it signs you in with your Zeiko Account.

  • Already signed in on this site? Drive opens straight away, signed in as you.
  • Not yet? Press Sign in with Zeiko Account. You sign in (or create an account) on this site, and come straight back to Drive.
  • One sign-in for everything. Sign out here or in Drive and you are signed out of both; sign in as somebody else and Drive follows.

Your email needs to be confirmed before Drive opens. The code is on its way when you sign up; you can ask for a new one on your account page.

Your first plan

Drive has no free plan: a plan is what lets you upload, and the smallest one is cheap enough to try. Plans are monthly, in rupiah, and the current ones are on the Drive page.

  1. Pick a planIn Drive, open Plans and press Choose on a plan.
  2. Pay for itDrive shows the amount and how to pay. The space is yours once the money arrives, usually within a few minutes, and the page carries on by itself.

How plans renew, move up or down, and what extra space costs is in Drive plans.

Uploading

  • Press New, then Upload files or Upload a folder, press + at the bottom right, or drag files onto the page.
  • Uploads run at full speed, several files at once, and can be paused and resumed. If the connection drops, Drive asks whether to pick up where it stopped.
  • When a name is already taken in that folder, you choose: Replace, Keep both or Skip — for one file or for all of them.
  • The biggest single file depends on your plan; it is written on each plan.

Finding and organising

PlaceWhat is there
My DriveYour own files and folders. Only you see them.
SharedEverything you shared with a link, and whether each link still works.
RecentWhat you opened or changed lately.
StarredFiles and folders you starred, to find them fast.
TrashWhat you deleted, until it is gone for good.

Search from the bar at the top, and filter by kind: folders, images, videos, audio, documents, PDFs or archives. Right-click a file, or open its menu, to rename, move, copy, star, preview, share or download it. Photos, videos, audio, PDFs and text files open in a preview.

The safety check

Every upload is checked before it can be shared. While that runs the file says Being checked; then it is Safe, or Blocked (harmful) and cannot be downloaded or shared. The check is automatic: no person opens your files.

Trash

Deleting moves a file to Trash, where you can Restore it. Trash says how many days a file stays there before it is deleted for good; Empty Trash does it at once.

Your data and your Drive

  • Language. Drive is in English and Indonesian; switch it in Settings. The choice is shared with this site.
  • Notifications. The bell in Drive always works; in Settings you choose which ones also come by email. Renewal reminders and receipts always do.
  • Your city. Local ads in Drive go by the city on your Zeiko Account, which sign-up asks for (the postal code is optional). Drive's Settings show it; change it in your account's Settings. Drive never guesses where you are.
  • Download your data. In Settings, Download my data gives a copy of your profile, file list, links and notifications; Download all my files gives a ZIP.
  • Delete your Drive. In Settings. Your Drive and its files are deleted for good a few days after you ask, and you can cancel until then. Your Zeiko Account stays.

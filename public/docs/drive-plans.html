Drive plans

Drive is paid by monthly plan, in rupiah. You pay for each thing when you buy it, in the app: there is no balance to fill first. The current plans and prices are on the Drive page.

On this page

  • Buying
  • Plans
  • Renewing
  • Moving up or down
  • Extra space and compress packs
  • When a plan is not renewed

Buying

  • Plans, extra space, compress packs and the next month of a plan are each paid when you buy them: press the button in Plans, and Drive shows the amount and how to pay. What you bought starts once the money arrives, usually within a few minutes, and the page carries on by itself.
  • There is no balance to fill first.
  • The amount to pay has a few rupiah added, so we can tell your transfer apart. They are not lost: Drive keeps them as credit and takes them off your next purchase. Plans shows your credit while you have any.
  • Drive is bought in Drive itself, in rupiah, apart from what you order in your Zeiko Account.

Plans

Each plan hasWhat it means
SpaceHow much you can keep, in GB (1 GB is 1,024 MB).
Largest fileThe biggest single file you can upload.
CompressionsPhoto and video compressions included each month.
PricePer month, in rupiah.

A plan starts as soon as it is paid and runs for a month.

Renewing

  • A plan does not renew by itself, unless your credit covers the next month. Drive emails you 3 days and 1 day before it ends.
  • Press Renew for a month in Plans any time before then: the new month starts when the current one ends, so renewing early loses nothing. Extra space you have not stopped renews with it.
  • If you have enough credit for the next month, Drive uses it and the plan renews on its own.

Moving up or down

  • Up: the bigger plan starts now, and you pay only the price difference for the days left in this month.
  • Down: your plan moves to the smaller one at the end of the month and stays as it is until then; you renew at the smaller plan's price. Cancel the downgrade keeps the plan you have.

Extra space and compress packs

  • Extra space adds GB on top of your plan and follows its month: you pay for the days left in it. Per GB it costs more than moving up a plan, and Drive says so beside it.
  • Compress packs add compress credits beyond those your plan includes. How compressing works is in Sharing & downloads.

When a plan is not renewed

If the month ends before you renew, Drive gives you time, and a banner says what to do. By default:

  1. On the day: graceEverything works. Renew and the plan carries on.
  2. After 3 days: read-onlyYou can still open, download and delete your files, but not upload, and your share links stop working.
  3. After 30 days: lockedYour files stay, but you cannot open them until the plan is renewed.
  4. After 37 days: deletedThe Drive and its files are deleted for good.

Renewing at any point before the last step brings everything back as it was, starting a new month from that day. Each step is announced first, in Drive and by email.

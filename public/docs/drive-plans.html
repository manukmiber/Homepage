Drive plans & balance

Drive is paid by monthly plan, in rupiah, from a Drive balance you top up in the app. The current plans and prices are on the Drive page.

On this page

  • Drive balance
  • Plans
  • Moving up or down
  • Extra space and compress packs
  • When a renewal does not go through

Drive balance

  • Plans, extra space, compress packs and local ads are paid from your Drive balance.
  • Add to it in Drive: Plans → Add balance. The app shows the amount and how to pay; your balance goes up once it arrives, usually within a few minutes, and the page carries on by itself.
  • Drive balance is in rupiah and is Drive's own: it is not the balance your Zeiko Account uses for Pranata V1 and Zeiko Chat. It can only be spent on Drive and cannot be taken out.
  • Balance history in Plans lists every amount in and out.

Plans

Each plan hasWhat it means
SpaceHow much you can keep, in GB (1 GB is 1,024 MB).
Largest fileThe biggest single file you can upload.
CompressionsPhoto and video compressions included each month.
PricePer month, in rupiah.

A plan starts as soon as you choose it and renews by itself each month from your Drive balance. Press Stop renewing to let it end at the end of the month; Keep renewing undoes that.

Moving up or down

  • Up: the bigger plan starts now, and you pay only the price difference for the days left in this month.
  • Down: your plan moves to the smaller one at the end of the month and stays as it is until then. Cancel the downgrade keeps the plan you have.

Extra space and compress packs

  • Extra space adds GB on top of your plan and follows its month. Per GB it costs more than moving up a plan, and Drive says so beside it.
  • Compress packs add compress credits beyond those your plan includes. How compressing works is in Sharing & downloads.

When a renewal does not go through

Drive reminds you a few days before a plan renews. If your balance is still short on the day, it gives you time, and a banner says what to do. By default:

  1. On the day: graceEverything works. Add balance and the plan renews.
  2. After 3 days: read-onlyYou can still open, download and delete your files, but not upload, and your share links stop working.
  3. After 30 days: lockedYour files stay, but you cannot open them until the plan is renewed.
  4. After 37 days: deletedThe Drive and its files are deleted for good.

Renewing at any point before the last step brings everything back as it was. Each step is announced first, in Drive, by email and on Telegram if you linked it.

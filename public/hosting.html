Questions, answered

Something else? Write to admin@zeiko.biz.id.

What can I host?

WordPress and other PHP sites, and apps of your own in Node.js, Python or Ruby. Each plan says how many websites it holds, from 1 on Mini to 50 on XXL.

How do I order?

Press Order on the plan you want: it starts an email to us with the plan already in it. Add monthly or yearly and your domain, send it, and we reply with the next steps.

Monthly or yearly?

Either. A year costs the same as ten months on every plan up to Extra Large, and less again on XXL.

Do I get SSH?

Yes, on every plan: jailed SSH, a shell of your own that is closed off from every other account.

Is SSL included?

Yes. Every site on every plan gets SSL, with nothing extra to buy.

Can I have email on my domain?

Yes. Make addresses like you@yourdomain.com in cPanel and read them in webmail or any mail app.

Are my files backed up?

We keep regular backups as a safety net, but they are not a promise: keep your own copy of your site too. cPanel can make one for you in a click.

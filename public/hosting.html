Questions, answered

Something else? Write to admin@zeiko.biz.id.

What can I host?

WordPress and other PHP sites, and apps of your own in Node.js, Python or Ruby. Each plan says how many websites it holds, from 1 on Mini to 20 on Extra Large.

How do I order?

Press Order on the plan you want. You sign in to your Zeiko Account (or make one), pick your address, choose monthly or yearly, and pay by QRIS. We check the payment in 1–5 minutes and make your hosting within 1–2 hours.

Do I need a domain?

No. Every hosting comes with a free address of its own under zeiko.biz.id, six random letters and digits. A name of your choosing in front of them is Rp3.000 per 3 months, and your own domain Rp10.000 per 3 months. You can change it later in your Zeiko Account: Rp5.000 once to move to your own domain.

How much traffic is included?

500 GB of egress a month on every plan. Past that, every GB is Rp10 (Rp5.000 per 500 GB), added to your next renewal. Hosting in your Zeiko Account shows how much you have used this month.

Monthly or yearly?

Either. A year costs the same as ten months. The lower first-month price of Mini, Small and Medium is for your first hosting with us, by the month; after that it is the plan's monthly price.

What if I forget to renew?

We email you 7, 3 and 1 day before your hosting ends, and you renew in your Zeiko Account. A day after it ends your site is suspended; a week after, the account is deleted with its files and databases. Renew any time before that and it carries on as it was.

Do I get SSH?

Yes, on every plan: jailed SSH, a shell of your own that is closed off from every other account.

Is SSL included?

Yes. Every site on every plan gets SSL, with nothing extra to buy.

Can I have email on my domain?

Yes. Make addresses like you@yourdomain.com in cPanel and read them in webmail or any mail app.

Are my files backed up?

We keep regular backups as a safety net, but they are not a promise: keep your own copy of your site too. cPanel can make one for you in a click.

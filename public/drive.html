    Questions, answered

    Something else? Chat us on WhatsApp +62 819-3241-6096 (08.00–22.00 WIB).

    Is there a free plan?

    No. Drive is paid, so your files are never the product — and the smallest plan is cheap enough to try without thinking twice.

    Do I need a new account?

    No. Drive uses your Zeiko Account — the same one as Pranata V1 and Zeiko Chat. Create one if you don’t have it yet.

    Who can see my files?

    Only you, and the people you send a link to. In a family plan, everyone’s My Drive stays private; only the Family Drive is shared.

    What if I run out of space?

    Move to a bigger plan, or add extra space for the rest of your period. Nothing is deleted when you are full; you just can’t upload more.

    What happens if a month isn’t renewed?

    We remind you before it ends. After that your files are read-only for a while, then locked, and only deleted weeks later — with reminders on the way. Renew at any point before then and everything comes back.

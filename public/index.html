    Help: questions

    Questions, answered

    Something else? Write to admin@zeiko.biz.id.

    How do I get Pranata V1?

    Create an account with your email or a one-click sign-in, then buy a plan or add a balance. Your key is waiting on your account page.

    What counts as one request on a Decision plan?

    A call of up to 1,024 tokens is one request; a call of 1,025 to 2,048 tokens is two. No call can be longer than 2,048 tokens.

    Does a Decision plan cover the roleplay models?

    No. Plans are for Pranata V1 only; Jagad, Asmarandana and Wisanggeni are priced per token.

    Which roleplay model should I pick?

    Jagad for safe-for-work roleplay, and when you are not sure. Asmarandana for adult roleplay, if you gate your own users. Wisanggeni when you need a model that rarely says no — it carries the highest rate. Compare them on one page.

    How is the cost of a roleplay request worked out?

    Per token, at the rates in the pricing table. Input, cache-read and output tokens are priced separately, and the output rate comes from the band your reasoning_effort lands in. Add them up and divide by a million.

    Do reasoning tokens count?

    Yes, as output tokens, at the output rate of the band the request landed in. Asking for less thinking lowers both the rate and the token count.

    What does a refused request cost?

    $0.05 flat for that request, not the price of a full answer.
